# The 1st CCF Cybersecurity Intelligence Challenge — “Jinghua Cup” TPCTF 2026

The 1st CCF Cybersecurity Intelligence Challenge, the “Jinghua Cup” TPCTF 2026, hosted by the China Computer Federation (CCF), is now open. Built around the idea of **humans and AI competing side by side**, it runs an **AI Agent Track** and a **Human-AI Collaboration Track** to explore how offence and defence change in the age of AI. Students and staff of universities across China and teams from cybersecurity companies are warmly invited to take part.

Universities are welcome to run their own on-campus awards alongside the event. Such awards are set by each university, are not part of this event's award system, and are not issued in CCF's name.

## 1. Background

AI is reshaping the balance of attack and defence in cybersecurity. Guided by national strategic needs, the event is **hosted by CCF**, **organised by the CCF Technical Committee on Network and System Security**, and **co-organised by Tsinghua University (Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace) and Peking University (Computer Center)**, with the aim of building a leading, internationally recognised cybersecurity competition.

For the first time it puts **humans and AI on the same field**: an **AI Agent Track** (agents compete on their own) and a **Human-AI Collaboration Track** (players compete together with AI). Through hands-on competition it seeks out the practical security talent the country needs and drives the development and application of security technology.

## 3. Tracks

There are two tracks; teams choose the one that suits them.

### AI Agent Track

- Teams of **up to 4 people from the same legal entity**.
- Each team writes an AI agent that follows the platform's specification and submits it to the competition system within the set time.
- The agent connects to the competition environment and **carries out every task on its own** under the published rules — both authoring and solving security challenges — **with no human intervention**.
- The track measures how well AI agents operate autonomously and precisely in complex network environments, and their potential for discovering and exploiting vulnerabilities.

### Human-AI Collaboration Track

- A **team** track, up to 4 members per team.
- Players work closely with AI tools throughout to solve complex security challenges, and may also **contribute challenges to the event** for extra incentive points.
- Any large model may be used; players can drive mainstream tools through agents (code generation, vulnerability analysis, reverse-engineering assistance and more).
- The track assesses how well players command AI and tooling, tests the limits of human-AI collaboration, and explores new ways of doing security work.

## 4. Who can enter

Full-time students of regular higher-education institutions (vocational colleges, undergraduate and postgraduate), and professionals working at cybersecurity companies. There is no restriction on field of study.

## 5. Challenge categories

The challenges keep CTF's hands-on core and add frontier areas such as AI security, testing everything from fundamentals to the latest attack and defence techniques.

**Offline (attachment) challenges**

- **Crypto**: attacking cryptographic schemes built on number-theoretic and algebraic problems
- **Reverse**: analysing and breaking program logic without source code
- **Misc**: steganography, digital forensics, classical ciphers and other mixed analysis

**Online (interactive) challenges**

- **Web**: finding and exploiting classic and emerging web vulnerabilities such as SQL injection and XSS
- **Pwn**: in-depth discovery and exploitation of low-level system and binary vulnerabilities
- **AI security**: finding and exploiting vulnerabilities in open-source AI frameworks and agents

## 8. Requirements

- **Platform**: the CTFy platform of the Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace, Tsinghua University.
- **Equipment**: teams provide their own stable network connection and the hardware and software they need; detailed requirements will be published in the participant handbook.
- **Rules**:
  1. AI Agent Track teams must submit their agent within the set time; no human intervention is allowed during the competition.
  2. The Human-AI Collaboration Track is a team track (up to 4 members per team); players must follow the platform rules throughout.
  3. Cheating of any kind is strictly forbidden and leads to disqualification.
